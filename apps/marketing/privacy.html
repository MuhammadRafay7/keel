Privacy policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

Not yet reviewed by a lawyer. This document describes honestly how Keel currently works, but it has not had legal review. Have it checked before relying on it commercially.

What this covers

This describes what the hosted Keel service at keel.ostenmark.com collects, why, and what happens to it. If you run your own copy of Keel, none of this applies to you — you control your own data.

What we collect

Account information

Your email address and password. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in readable form.

What you put into Keel

Work items, comments, pages, attachments, project and workspace names — whatever you and your team create while using the product.

Operational records

Standard server logs, including IP address and timestamps, kept to keep the service running and to investigate abuse.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your content to train machine learning models.
  • We do not run advertising, and we do not share your data with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Keel stores data in a managed PostgreSQL database provided by Supabase, hosted in the European Union. Files are stored in Supabase Storage. The application is served by Vercel.

Who can see your data

Access is enforced in the database itself through row-level security, so a member of one workspace cannot read another workspace's data. Ostenmark staff access production data only when needed to operate or repair the service.

How long we keep it

Your content is kept while your account is active. Delete your account and we remove your personal data and content, except where we must keep records to meet a legal obligation. Backups roll off on their own schedule.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy@ostenmark.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Keel sets a session cookie so you stay signed in. There are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and change the date at the top.
