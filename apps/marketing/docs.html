Concepts

Work items

The unit everything else hangs off. An item carries state, priority, assignees, labels, estimates and dates. Items nest into sub-items and link to each other as blocking, duplicate or related.

Cycles

Time-boxed periods, the equivalent of sprints. A cycle has a start and end date and tracks burn-down as work completes. Finished cycles archive.

Modules

Durable groupings that cut across cycles, for splitting a large project into shippable pieces.

Views

A saved combination of filters, grouping and layout. Views can be private to you or shared with the project.

Pages

Collaborative rich-text documents. Several people can edit the same page at once.

Intake

A triage inbox. Requests land here first and are accepted, declined, snoozed or merged before reaching the backlog.

Layouts

Work items render as list, board, calendar, spreadsheet or timeline. Any layout can be grouped and filtered by any property, and the result saved as a view.

Running your own copy

Keel is AGPL-licensed, so you can host it yourself. Requirements and setup are in the README. If you modify Keel and serve it over a network, AGPL §13 obliges you to offer your users the source of your version.
